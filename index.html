        Engine

        This hosted deck thinks with Claude, on your own Claude account. To run it on a private model on your own hardware, use the standalone file: it has the same interface and connects to Ollama, LM Studio, llama.cpp or any OpenAI-style server.

        Works with Ollama (http://localhost:11434/v1), LM Studio (:1234/v1), llama.cpp, vLLM, or anything that speaks /chat/completions. Also works with hosted gateways such as OpenRouter (https://openrouter.ai/api/v1); with a hosted gateway your messages go to that service and the model's provider. Free models only applies where the service lists prices.
